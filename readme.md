# talkboxes

talkboxes was an art installation.
it consisted of 2 keyboard-boxes at opposite ends of the art department.
pressing keys on either box would light up the corresponding keys on the other box.
it let people communicate anonymously and spontaneously.
there were no instructions.

[![talkboxes.mp4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crittergirl/talkboxes/main/talkboxes_thumbnail.png)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crittergirl/talkboxes/main/talkboxes.mp4)

## how it worked

each box was an arduino mega wired up to 26 buttons and leds.
the boxes were wired together with a half-duplex serial line.

## inspiration

talkboxes was inspired by spontaneous nonverbal communication in multiplayer videogames.
a good example is crouch-spamming in minecraft.
it's often a sign of friendliness or peace, but that depends on context.
the important part is that it isn't negotiated beforehand.
